Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

What timber do you use?

Whatever's right for the job — European oak for most cabinetry and stairs, tulipwood for paint-grade, accoya for exterior. Happy to talk options.

Do you do listed buildings?

Yes — used to working with conservation officers and matching period detail. We'll talk you through what's likely to be approved.

Costs?

Hard to ballpark without seeing it, but bespoke joinery typically starts around £2,000 for a small commission and goes up from there.

Lead times?

Usually 6–10 weeks from sign-off, depending on workshop load and timber availability.

Can you provide drawings?

Yes — sketches as standard, CAD if the job warrants it. Drawings stay your property.
