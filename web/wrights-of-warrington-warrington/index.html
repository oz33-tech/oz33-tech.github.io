Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

Do you do design or just maintenance?

Both. Some clients want a full redesign and planting plan, others just want their garden looked after well. We do whichever you need.

Costs?

Maintenance from £40 per visit. Bigger projects (new borders, patios, fencing) quoted itemised — most landscaping jobs land between £1,500 and £15,000.

When's the best time to plant?

Autumn for shrubs and trees, spring for tender plants. We'll plan around the right season for what you're planting.

Will you take the garden waste away?

Yes — all green waste removed and properly disposed of. Included in the price.

Are you insured?

Yes — public liability and contractor insurance. Happy to send the certificate.
