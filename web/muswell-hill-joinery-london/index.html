Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

Do you build in a workshop or on site?

Both. Carcasses and bigger pieces are built in the workshop where we've got the proper tools, then fitted on site. Smaller work is done in place.

Can you match existing mouldings?

Usually yes — most period mouldings can be matched by a router or sourced from specialist suppliers. We'll tell you straight at the quote.

What does fitted storage cost?

A typical alcove cupboard with shelves and doors lands between £900 and £1,800 depending on size, finish and any special features.

Do you handle the painting?

Most clients have us prime everything and either we finish, or a painter does the topcoat. We can recommend a painter we work with.

Lead time?

Usually 3–6 weeks from sign-off to fitting. Bigger builds longer.
