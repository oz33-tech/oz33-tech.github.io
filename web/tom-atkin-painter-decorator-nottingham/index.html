Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

How long does a typical room take?

A standard living room is usually 2–3 days including prep. Bigger jobs scale from there. We'll give you a realistic schedule with the quote.

What does it cost?

Most interior rooms land between £400 and £900 depending on prep and ceiling height. Exteriors vary widely with access and condition.

Can I stay in the house while you work?

Yes, we're set up for it — low-odour paints, dust sheets, daily clean-up. Most clients carry on as normal.

Do you supply the paint?

Yes, included in the quote. We use trade-grade Dulux, Crown or Farrow & Ball depending on the job. Happy to use a brand you prefer.

Do you do small repairs first?

Yes — filling, sanding and minor plaster repairs are part of the prep, not an extra.
