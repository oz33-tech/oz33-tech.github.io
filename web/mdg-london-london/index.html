Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

How long is the lead time?

Usually 3–6 weeks from order to fitting, depending on frame type. Bespoke and conservation profiles longer.

Costs?

Standard UPVC casements £450–£800 fitted. Sashes and aluminium £900–£1,800. Bifolds quoted per metre of opening.

Will you handle building control notification?

Yes — FENSA notification included as standard. You get a certificate for your home pack.

Do you do conservation areas / listed buildings?

Yes — slim-line double-glazed sashes and timber alternatives that meet conservation guidance.

What's the guarantee?

10 years on frames, 5 years on glass sealed units, 1 year on fit. Written guarantee on completion.
