Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

How quickly can you get scaffold up?

Most domestic jobs we can erect within 5–10 working days of quote sign-off. Urgent jobs we'll do our best.

Costs?

Most domestic scaffolds £400–£1,200 for a 2-week hire. Bigger structures, chimney scaffolds and commercial work quoted itemised.

Do you handle road permits?

Yes — pavement and highway licences applied for and managed as part of the job.

Are you fully insured?

Yes — public liability, employer's liability, and design liability. Certificates available on request.

Tied or independent?

Depends on the building. We'll work out the right design at the site visit.
