Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

How long does it take?

A standard loft boarding job is 1–2 days. Adding insulation + ladder + lights pushes it to 2–3 days. I'll give you a real timeline with the quote.

Will boarding squash my insulation?

No — I use a raised-leg system that sits above the joists. Your insulation stays at full depth underneath, doing its job. Standard boarding straight onto joists compresses insulation by ~50% and most people don't realise.

Building Regs?

Loft insulation needs to be 270mm to meet current standards. I top up to that as standard. The boards, ladder and lights aren't notifiable but I do them to the same spec.

How much weight can the boards take?

Properly installed boarded loft holds 350kg per square metre — that's heavy storage, christmas trees, suitcases, the lot. I'll show you the spec sheet.

Do you do the ladder too?

Yes — ladder, hatch, lights and boards are usually done as one job. Cheaper that way and tidier than getting separate trades in.
