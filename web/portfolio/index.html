Hi, I'm Omar. I build websites for UK trades and small businesses — usually one page, always custom-coded, never a template builder hiding under a coat of paint.

I work alone. That keeps prices honest and lets me actually pick up the phone when a client needs something changed. No project managers, no account team, no monthly retainer with vague "deliverables" — just the person who built your site, available when you need them.
