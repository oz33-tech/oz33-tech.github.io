Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

How quickly can you come out?

For non-emergency jobs we're usually booking 1–2 weeks ahead. Emergencies (burst pipes, no heat in winter) we try to fit in same-day.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes — fully registered and insured. Happy to show you the card and certificate before we start anything gas-related.

What does a new boiler cost?

Most straight swaps land between £2,000 and £3,500 including the boiler, flue, fittings and a system flush. We'll give you a fixed price after a quick look.

Do you do bathroom installations?

Yes — full bathrooms or single fixtures. We handle the plumbing and coordinate the tiling, electrics and plastering if needed.

Do you offer landlord gas certificates?

Yes, CP12s done while we're on site. Paperwork emailed the same day.
