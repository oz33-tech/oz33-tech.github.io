Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

Lime mortar vs cement?

On period buildings, lime — always. Cement traps moisture in soft stone and accelerates decay. We mix the right lime for the building's age and exposure.

Costs?

Repointing typically £40–£90 per m². Indents priced per piece. New work quoted itemised. Heritage work is slower and reflects in the price.

Can you match existing stone?

Usually yes — local sandstone, limestone or granite from the original quarry where it's still active, or a close match where it isn't.

Do you work on listed buildings?

Yes. We'll work with your conservation officer and prepare statements of works if needed for listed building consent.

Lead times?

Heritage work usually 6–12 weeks out. Smaller jobs sooner.
