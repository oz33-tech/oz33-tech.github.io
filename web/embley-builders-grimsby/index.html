Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

Do you do design as well?

We can introduce you to architects we trust, or work to drawings you already have. Either way, the build is on us.

Costs?

Extensions typically £1,800–£2,800 per m² fitted, depending on spec. Loft conversions £40,000–£75,000. Full refurbs vary hugely.

Will you handle planning and building control?

Yes — we coordinate planning submissions and manage building control sign-off as part of the project.

Stage payments?

Yes — tied to milestones, never paid in advance. Final balance held until snags are complete.

Guarantees?

10-year structural guarantee on extensions, 6-year on refurbs. Written, on letterhead.
