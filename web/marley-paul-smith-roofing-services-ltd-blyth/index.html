Common questions

The things people usually ask first.

Anything we haven't covered — just call or drop us an email.

Do I need a full re-roof or just a repair?

Often a repair is enough. We'll tell you straight — if 80% of the roof has life left, a re-roof is throwing money away.

Costs?

Repairs from £150–£600. Re-roofs of a typical semi between £6,000 and £12,000 depending on tile choice and access.

Do you handle scaffolding?

Yes — included in the quote. One job, one invoice.

Will my insurance cover storm damage?

Most policies do. We can survey it, write a report and quote, and deal with the insurer's surveyor if needed.

How long does a re-roof take?

A typical semi is 4–7 working days, weather permitting. We'll give you a realistic schedule.
